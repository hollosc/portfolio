Advertising, Branding & Marketing Communication

Csaba Hollós is an award-winning creative professional with more than two decades of experience in advertising, branding and marketing communication. His portfolio includes integrated campaigns, television commercials, brand development projects, digital experiences and creative concepts developed for international and local brands.

Throughout his career he has worked with leading agencies including BBDO, Young & Rubicam and HPS Experience, contributing to campaigns recognized by Cannes Lions, Aranypenge, Effie and Hipnózis awards. His work combines strategic thinking, brand building and creative execution across multiple communication channels.

This portfolio presents a selection of advertising campaigns, TV commercials, brand communication projects and creative work developed for clients across FMCG, automotive, telecommunications, retail and cultural sectors.
